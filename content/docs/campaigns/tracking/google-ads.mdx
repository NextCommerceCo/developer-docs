## What it does

YouTube ads are bought and measured through Google Ads, so this one guide covers both. Nothing on this page is specific to YouTube.

NEXT has no dedicated Google Ads integration for campaign funnels. The starter templates have no Google Ads key or adapter, and no NEXT store app sends Google Ads conversions from the server for campaign orders. What NEXT does provide:

- The SDK captures the Google click ID (`gclid`) from the ad's landing URL and stores it on the order.
- The GA4 adapter and the SDK's GTM provider send each purchase and accepted upsell from the funnel with the order number, value and currency.

You get those purchases into Google Ads in one of two ways:

| Path | How it works | Guide you set up first |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Import GA4 key events | The GA4 adapter sends `purchase` and `upsell_purchase` to your GA4 property. You mark them as key events and import them into Google Ads as conversions | [Google Analytics 4](/docs/campaigns/tracking/google-analytics-4) |
| Google Ads tags in your GTM container | Your own GTM container listens for the SDK's `dl_purchase` and `dl_upsell_purchase` events and fires your Google Ads conversion tags | [Google Tag Manager](/docs/campaigns/tracking/google-tag-manager) |

Use one path for each conversion, not both. Running both counts every purchase twice.

These are not available:

- A dedicated Google Ads key in `_data/campaigns.json` or a Google Ads adapter.
- Enhanced conversions. Nothing in the templates or the SDK sends hashed customer data to Google Ads.
- Server-side conversion upload. Storing `gclid` on the order does not send anything to Google Ads, and NEXT has no feature that uploads orders or click IDs to Google Ads.

<Callout type="warn" title="The Google Analytics 4 store app does not reach funnels">
The [Google Analytics 4 store app](https://docs.nextcommerce.com/docs/apps/google-analytics-4) has its own Google Ads conversion settings. That app runs on your NEXT storefront only and does not load on a campaign funnel, so those settings do nothing for campaign orders.
</Callout>

## Before you start

- A Google Ads account with auto-tagging turned on. Without auto-tagging, ad clicks carry no `gclid`.
- A funnel built from a starter template, with your funnel domain added in the Campaigns app under **Settings**, then **Allowed Domains**, as a **Production** domain. Analytics and event tracking are ignored on development domains.
- For the GA4 import path:
  - GA4 set up on the funnel and working. Follow [Google Analytics 4](/docs/campaigns/tracking/google-analytics-4) first.
  - Your Google Analytics and Google Ads accounts linked.
  - Administrator or editor access to the GA4 property. Google's help says you need at least Marketer access to import.
- For the GTM path:
  - Your own GTM container loading on the funnel with the SDK's GTM provider on. Follow [Google Tag Manager](/docs/campaigns/tracking/google-tag-manager) first.
  - A Google Ads conversion action for purchases, and one for upsells if you want to count them separately.

## Set up

<Tabs items={['Starter template (Page Kit)', 'Hand-built funnel']}>
<Tab value="Starter template (Page Kit)">

### Import GA4 key events

1. Set up the GA4 adapter with your measurement ID (`ga4_id`) as described in [Google Analytics 4](/docs/campaigns/tracking/google-analytics-4#set-up), and check that `purchase` arrives.
2. In GA4, mark `purchase` as a key event. Mark `upsell_purchase` too if you want upsell revenue to count in Google Ads.
3. Link your Google Analytics property to your Google Ads account, and turn on auto-tagging in Google Ads.
4. In GA4, open **Advertising**, then **Tools**, then **Conversion management**, and select **New conversion**. Pick your Google Ads account, pick the key events, choose a conversion category and save. Events you pick from the events list in this flow are marked as key events. Google's steps are in [Create Google Ads conversions from GA4 key events](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10632359?hl=en).
5. In Google Ads, check how the new conversion actions are set to count. Google's help explains why a conversion created this way can be marked as a secondary conversion action. Secondary actions are not used for bidding until you change them.

Google notes that this feature may not be available to every Google Analytics property.

### Google Ads tags in your GTM container

1. Load your GTM container on the funnel and turn on the SDK's GTM provider, as described in [Google Tag Manager](/docs/campaigns/tracking/google-tag-manager#set-up). The SDK does not load GTM itself.
2. In GTM, add data layer variables for `ecommerce.transaction_id`, `ecommerce.value` and `ecommerce.currency`.
3. Add a trigger that fires on the `dl_purchase` event.
4. Add a Google Ads conversion tag for your purchase conversion action. Set its transaction ID, value and currency from the variables in step 2, and attach the `dl_purchase` trigger.
5. To count upsells, add a second trigger on `dl_upsell_purchase` and a second Google Ads conversion tag that uses it.
6. Publish the container, build the funnel with `npm run build`, and deploy. The analytics markup is left out of `npm run dev` builds.

If you use this path, do not also import the GA4 `purchase` key event for the same conversion.

## Events sent

Google Ads receives nothing from NEXT directly. It receives what your GA4 property or your GTM tags pass on.

| Funnel event | GA4 import path | GTM path | When it fires |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `dl_purchase` | GA4 `purchase`, imported as a conversion | Trigger for your purchase conversion tag | The first page after payment (upsell or receipt), once per order |
| `dl_upsell_purchase` | GA4 `upsell_purchase`, imported as a conversion if you choose | Trigger for your upsell conversion tag, if you add one | The page after an upsell is accepted |

The other funnel events (page views and checkout steps) also reach GA4 and your GTM container. See their guides for the full lists. This guide covers purchase conversions only.

## Attribution requirements

- Auto-tagging must be on in Google Ads so the landing URL carries `gclid`.
- The SDK reads `gclid` from the landing URL, keeps it for the browser tab session, and sends it on the order as `attribution.gclid`. A new `gclid` on a later page replaces the old one.
- The SDK carries the query string through the checkout, upsell and receipt pages, so Google's tags on those pages see the same URL parameters as the landing page.
- Keep every funnel page on one domain, or on subdomains of one domain, so the GA4 cookie is shared across the funnel. See [Google Analytics 4](/docs/campaigns/tracking/google-analytics-4#attribution-requirements).
- SDK 0.4.x does not store `gbraid` or `wbraid` on the order. It keeps them in the funnel's page links, but they are not in the order's attribution. SDK 0.5.0 stores both in the order's attribution metadata.

### Shop Sync stores

On a store that uses Shop Sync, campaign orders are copied into Shopify. A Google Ads conversion tracked on the Shopify side, for example by a Shopify Google app, does not see the funnel visit, because the shopper never loaded a Shopify page.

Shop Sync does not copy `gclid` to the Shopify order on its own. According to the [Shop Sync metadata guide](https://guides.nextcommerce.com/guides/shop-sync-metadata), it reaches the Shopify order only when:

1. A metadata field for it exists in NEXT, under **Settings**, then **Metadata Fields**, on the attribution object. See [Metadata fields and tags](https://docs.nextcommerce.com/docs/build-a-store/technical-settings/metadata-fields-and-tags).
2. The value is in the order's attribution metadata. The guide notes that `gclid` is a standard attribution field rather than metadata, so it has to be copied into metadata first, with the SDK's `next.addMetadata('gclid', ...)`.
3. The field is mapped to a Shopify order metafield in Shop Sync with **Add Metadata Sync**. The types must match, for example a NEXT text field to a Shopify single line text field.

Metafields are written once, when the Shopify order is created. This mapping is Unconfirmed: NEXT has not walked it. See [Tracking on Shop Sync stores](/docs/campaigns/tracking/shop-sync) for which Shopify-side tools see synced orders.

## Purchases, revenue and upsells

| Field | Order purchase | Upsell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Event | `dl_purchase`, sent to GA4 as `purchase` | `dl_upsell_purchase`, sent to GA4 as `upsell_purchase` |
| `transaction_id` | The NEXT order number, for example `100041` | The order reference plus `-US1`, `-US2` and so on |
| `value` | Item revenue, excluding tax and shipping | The upsell line only |
| `currency` | The order currency | The order currency |

Upsells are sent as separate events. If you import only `purchase`, or add only a `dl_purchase` tag, Google Ads counts the first sale and not the upsell revenue. Because `value` excludes tax and shipping, Google Ads conversion value will be lower than the order total.

`dl_purchase` is sent once per order in a browser. A reload or revisit of the page does not send it again.

## Browser and server events

Google Ads gets campaign conversions from the browser only, through GA4 or your GTM tags. NEXT sends no Google Ads events from the server, so there is no server event to deduplicate against.

Send each purchase from one place: either the GA4 import or a GTM conversion tag, and not a Shopify-side Google app on top. See [Send each purchase from one place](/docs/campaigns/tracking#send-each-purchase-from-one-place).

## Worked example

A Google Ads click to a funnel that uses the GA4 import path. The campaign entry sets only GA4:

```json title="_data/campaigns.json"
{
  "summer-offer": {
    "name": "Summer Offer",
    "sdk_version": "0.4.43",
    "ga4_id": "G-XXXXXXXXXX"
  }
}
```

With auto-tagging on, a shopper lands on `https://offers.example.com/presell/?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=summer-offer&gclid=EXAMPLE_GCLID`, buys the $12 main offer and accepts a $3 upsell.

| Where | What you see |
| --- | --- |
| The NEXT order | `attribution.gclid` is `EXAMPLE_GCLID`, with `utm_source`, `utm_medium` and `utm_campaign` alongside it |
| GA4, upsell page | `purchase` with `transaction_id` `100041` and `value` 12 |
| GA4, receipt page | `upsell_purchase` with `transaction_id` set to the order reference plus `-US1` and `value` 3 |
| Google Ads | One conversion for `purchase`, plus one for `upsell_purchase` if you imported it (Unconfirmed) |

## Verify it works

1. Open a funnel page on your production domain with a test click ID on the URL, for example `?gclid=EXAMPLE_GCLID&nfdebug=true`. Do not add `?ignore=true`, which turns all tracking off for the browser tab.
2. In the browser console, run `next.getAttribution()`. It should show your `gclid`.
3. Place a test order with a [test card](/docs/testing) and accept an upsell.
4. Check the order's attribution, for example through the Admin API. `attribution.gclid` should hold your test value.
5. For the GA4 path, confirm `purchase` and `upsell_purchase` as described in [Google Analytics 4](/docs/campaigns/tracking/google-analytics-4#verify-it-works).
6. For the GTM path, use Google Tag Assistant to confirm your conversion tags fire on `dl_purchase` and `dl_upsell_purchase` with the order number, value and currency.

<Callout type="warn" title="Test orders send real events">
The SDK does not check whether an order is a test order. A test order sends `purchase` to your GA4 property and fires your GTM tags like a real one, so it can be counted in Google Ads through either path.
</Callout>

More checks are in [Test and troubleshoot tracking](/docs/campaigns/tracking/testing-and-troubleshooting).

## Troubleshooting and limits

| Symptom | Likely cause | Fix |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No `gclid` on the order | Auto-tagging is off, or the ad's URL drops the query string before the funnel loads | Turn on auto-tagging; check the landing URL in the browser carries `gclid` |
| Every conversion counted twice | Both paths run for the same conversion, or a Shopify-side Google app also counts the synced order | Keep one sender per conversion |
| Upsell revenue missing in Google Ads | `upsell_purchase` was not imported, or there is no `dl_upsell_purchase` tag | Import the key event or add the tag |
| Conversion value lower than the order total | `value` excludes tax and shipping by design | Compare against item revenue in NEXT |
| Imported conversion not used for bidding | It was created as a secondary conversion action | Change it in Google Ads |
| Cannot create a conversion from GA4 | Missing access, unlinked accounts, or the feature is not available to the property | Check the prerequisites in [Google's help](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/10632359?hl=en) |
| Google Ads settings in the Google Analytics 4 store app have no effect | That app runs on the storefront only | Use one of the two paths on this page |

- There is no Google Ads key or adapter in the starter templates.
- There are no enhanced conversions and no server-side conversion upload from NEXT.
- NEXT has not verified that imported conversions or GTM conversion tags report correctly in Google Ads.
- SDK 0.4.x does not store `gbraid` or `wbraid` on the order.
- An upsell accepted as a bundle sends one `dl_upsell_purchase` per item in the bundle, each valued at the whole bundle total (from SDK source, not walked). A conversion tag or imported key event counts each of them.
- Google Ads receives no conversion for subscription renewals or other orders created without a funnel visit.
