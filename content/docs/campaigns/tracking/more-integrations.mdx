Most platforms here are turned on with one line in `_data/campaigns.json`. Add the line to your campaign, then build with `npm run build` and deploy. See [Set up tracking](/docs/campaigns/tracking/getting-started) for the full steps.

| Platform | Line to add |
| --- | --- |
| [TikTok](#tiktok) | `tiktok_pixel_id` |
| [Snapchat](#snapchat) | `snap_pixel_id` |
| [Pinterest](#pinterest) | `pinterest_tag_id` |
| [Taboola](#taboola) | `taboola_account_id` |
| [Northbeam](#northbeam) | `northbeam_client_id` |
| [RudderStack](#rudderstack) | Two lines and a setting |
| [Klaviyo](#klaviyo) | A store app |

[Everflow](/docs/campaigns/tracking/everflow) and [AppLovin Axon](/docs/campaigns/tracking/applovin-axon) have their own guides.

## TikTok

```json
"tiktok_pixel_id": "your-pixel-id"
```

- **What it tracks:** page views, product views, add to cart, checkout started, payment details and purchases.
- **Upsells:** each accepted upsell counts as another purchase, so an order with two upsells shows as three purchases. To count one purchase per order, add `"tiktok_blocked_events": "dl_upsell_purchase"`. Upsell revenue then does not reach TikTok.
- **Shopper details:** add `"tiktok_advanced_matching_enabled": "true"` to send email and phone. Nothing is sent unless the marketing opt-in checkbox on your checkout is ticked.

The TikTok store app covers your NEXT storefront, not funnels.

## Snapchat

```json
"snap_pixel_id": "your-pixel-id"
```

- **What it tracks:** page views, product views, add to cart, checkout started, payment details and purchases.
- **Upsells:** each accepted upsell counts as another purchase.
- **Shopper details:** add `"snap_advanced_matching_enabled": "true"` to send email and phone. Nothing is sent unless the marketing opt-in checkbox on your checkout is ticked.

## Pinterest

```json
"pinterest_tag_id": "your-tag-id"
```

- **What it tracks:** page views, add to cart, and a `checkout` event for each order.
- **Upsells:** each accepted upsell counts as another `checkout`.
- **Shopper details:** add `"pinterest_enhanced_match_enabled": "true"` to send email. Nothing is sent unless the marketing opt-in checkbox on your checkout is ticked.

## Taboola

```json
"taboola_account_id": "1234567"
```

Use your numeric account ID.

- **What it tracks:** page views, product views, add to cart, checkout started, payment details and purchases.
- **Upsells:** sent as a separate `upsell_purchase` event. Create a Taboola conversion for it to count upsell revenue.

The **Taboola Pixel Events** store app covers your NEXT storefront, not funnels.

## Northbeam

```json
"northbeam_client_id": "your-client-id"
```

- **What it tracks:** page views, and a purchase for each order.
- **Upsells:** each accepted upsell counts as another purchase.
- **Shopper details:** add `"northbeam_identity_enabled": "true"` to send the shopper's email, which helps Northbeam match orders.

### Northbeam on a Shop Sync store

Northbeam already receives your orders from Shopify. Stop the funnel sending them too, and turn identity on:

```json
"northbeam_client_id": "your-client-id",
"northbeam_identity_enabled": "true",
"northbeam_blocked_events": "dl_purchase, dl_upsell_purchase"
```

## RudderStack

RudderStack needs two lines and one setting.

```json
"rudderstack_write_key": "your-write-key",
"rudderstack_dataplane_url": "https://your-dataplane-url"
```

Then switch `rudderstack` on in `assets/config.js`:

```js title="assets/config.js"
// Inside analytics.providers:
rudderstack: {
  enabled: true
},
```

RudderStack is a pipe, like Google Tag Manager. It reports nothing by itself; the destinations you connect in RudderStack do. It receives the shopper's details along with the events, and each accepted upsell arrives as another `Order Completed`.

## Klaviyo

Klaviyo is a store app, not a line in your funnel. Once installed, it sends order events to Klaviyo from NEXT's servers for funnel orders and storefront orders alike. See the [Klaviyo app docs](https://docs.nextcommerce.com/docs/apps/klaviyo).
